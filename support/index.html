About Vaanii

Vaanii helps children learn the Gujarati alphabet through interactive mini-games, quizzes, and letter-tracing activities. It is designed for young learners at home and in the classroom.

The app works fully offline, contains no advertising, and requires no account or sign-in. Everything a child creates stays on the device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Getting started

Do I need to create an account or sign in?

No. Vaanii has no accounts, logins, or passwords. You create a profile for your child directly in the app and start learning right away.

Does Vaanii need an internet connection?

No. Vaanii works completely offline. Once the app is installed, no internet connection is required to play the games, take quizzes, or track progress.

Can more than one child use the same device?

Yes. You can create a separate profile for each child, and each profile keeps its own learning progress, game history, and quiz results independently.

Is there any advertising in the app?

No. Vaanii contains no advertisements of any kind, and no third-party advertising or tracking software.

Progress and data

Where is my child’s data stored?

Entirely on your own device, inside the app’s private storage. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete a child’s profile or data?

You can delete an individual profile from within the app, which removes that profile’s data from the device.

To remove everything, uninstall the app. Uninstalling permanently deletes all locally stored data, including every profile, all learning progress, and all gameplay history.

How do I move a child’s progress to a new device?

Use the app’s backup and export feature to export the child’s learning data to a file, transfer that file to the new device, and import it there.

Do this before uninstalling the app or resetting the old device — once the app is removed, the data cannot be recovered.

Is my child’s progress backed up automatically?

No. Because Vaanii stores everything on the device and never uploads it, there is no automatic cloud backup. If you want a copy of your child’s progress, export it using the backup feature and keep the file somewhere safe.

Can I correct a name or roll number I entered wrongly?

Yes. Profile details can be edited directly in the app at any time.

For teachers and schools

Can Vaanii be used in a classroom?

Yes. Vaanii is built with classroom use in mind. Each student can have their own profile with a name and roll number, and teachers can review progress on the device or export it for record-keeping.

Does student data leave the device or go to a server?

No. In the current release, no student data is transmitted anywhere. All information stays on the device it was entered on. Our Privacy Policy describes exactly what is stored and how.

We’re a school interested in deploying Vaanii. Who do we talk to?

Email us at contactus@dreamcraftersinnovations.com and tell us a little about your school and how many students you have in mind. We’re happy to help with rollout and answer procurement or data-protection questions.

Troubleshooting

Before you reinstall: deleting the app permanently erases all profiles and learning progress on that device. If the data matters, export a backup first — try the steps below before removing the app.

The app won’t open, or closes unexpectedly

  1. Fully close the app (swipe it away from the app switcher) and open it again.
  2. Restart your device.
  3. Check the App Store for a Vaanii update and install it if one is available.
  4. Make sure your device has some free storage space available.
  5. If it still won’t open, email us with your device model and iOS version.

Audio isn’t playing

  1. Check that your device isn’t on silent, and turn the volume up.
  2. Disconnect any Bluetooth headphones or speakers that may be capturing the sound.
  3. Close and reopen the app.

Progress or a profile seems to be missing

  1. Check whether a different profile is selected — each child’s progress is stored separately under their own profile.
  2. Confirm the app was not uninstalled and reinstalled, which erases all stored data.
  3. If you exported a backup earlier, import it to restore that child’s progress.
  4. Contact us if the data still doesn’t appear, and tell us what you were doing when it went missing.

Tracing or touch controls aren’t responding

  1. Clean the screen and remove any thick screen protector or case edge that overlaps the display.
  2. Close and reopen the app.
  3. Restart the device.
